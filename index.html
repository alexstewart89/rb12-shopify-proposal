1What the current website cannot fix

rbtwelve.com runs a WordPress front end with WooCommerce for checkout, Stripe and PayPal bolted on, and Klaviyo for email. Performance plugins have been added this year and the pages load better. None of that changes the points below, which came up on our previous calls and still hold.

Paid traffic is being run blind

Today: ad spend is decided without seeing which products get added to basket, where visitors drop out, or what each product actually converts at. Roughly £3k a month in ads is being judged on one number at the end.

Shopify reports add-to-basket, checkout started and conversion by product, channel and landing page out of the box, and feeds Meta and Google through native pixels, so spend can be moved to what sells.

Revenue · paid efficiency

The checkout is the weakest page on the site

Today: the right people reach the site and do not buy. A WooCommerce checkout on a £2,000 sofa gives them every reason to pause and come back later, or not at all.

Shopify Checkout with Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna on one page. Shopify's checkout converts on average 15% better than other platforms (independent study, 2023, sourced below). On high-ticket furniture, saved details and finance at the point of decision matter more, not less.

Revenue · conversion

5,000 products with fabric, leg, depth and orientation options

Today: the catalogue is the reason the move stalled last time. It is also the reason the current site is hard to keep accurate, because every supplier update is a manual edit across plugins.

On Shopify the catalogue is one structured product file with options, metafields and bulk editing. Migration from WooCommerce is routine work for a Shopify Partner with the right tooling, with the mapping reviewed before anything is imported. It is not a CSV-and-hope job, and it should not sit with a marketing lead.

Catalogue · accuracy

The showroom and the website are two businesses

Today: around four fifths of revenue is taken in Victoria, and the online store has no idea it happened. A customer who sat on the sofa on Saturday is an anonymous visitor online on Sunday.

Shopify POS in the showroom puts the customer, the quote and the order on the same record as the website. Staff build the order at the sample wall, send a payment link, and the sale is attributed properly whichever way it closes.

Showroom · one customer record

Big tickets are being paid by bank transfer

Today: customers on multi-thousand-pound orders are pushed towards bank transfer to avoid card fees, which means manual reconciliation, chasing, and a quote that quietly goes cold after the 14-day validity window.

Draft orders with a pay-by-link invoice, card or bank transfer, expiry built in, and automatic reconciliation into Shopify. The quote and the follow-up live in one place rather than in a sent-items folder.

Cash · less admin

Every improvement depends on a specialist

Today: theme, plugins, PHP, hosting and security patches are RB12's problem. Each conversion idea is a developer task or an AI-assisted edit on a stack nobody owns end to end.

Shopify hosts, patches and scales the store. Page changes are drag and drop for the team or the agency, apps install in minutes, and the roadmap arrives automatically, including products becoming discoverable inside AI assistants as that rolls out.

Operations · less dependency

2What RB12 could be doing instead

Two sides of the same catalogue. Online does the research, the showroom does the reassurance, and the order can close in either place.

Online: rbtwelve.com as a proper sales channel

A product page that configures fabric, legs, depth and orientation cleanly, with lead times and samples visible. Klaviyo connected natively for browse and basket recovery on considered purchases. Klarna and Shop Pay Installments where it helps close. Product and landing-page level reporting so paid budget follows what sells. The target discussed on our previous calls was £10k to £30k a month online, against roughly £6.7k today; the platform is built for that, the current one is not.

In the showroom: POS Pro for Victoria

Staff look up the customer, see what they browsed online, build the quote at the sample wall and take payment on a card reader or send a pay link. Orders ship direct from the supplier as today, with the customer notified from the same system. Exchanges, deposits and staff permissions handled properly. Delivery and white-glove installation added as line items, not as emails.

5The catalogue, dealt with properly

Mapped before it is moved

A partner exports the WooCommerce catalogue, maps attributes to Shopify options and metafields, and the Shopify team reviews the structure before a test import on a development store. Nothing touches a live site until the product pages look right.

Suppliers stay as they are

RB12 does not hold stock, so orders continue to route to suppliers. That flow is set up once, either through the supplier's own Shopify integration or a simple automation, rather than rebuilt by hand per order.

Klaviyo and ads carry over

Klaviyo, Meta, Google, TikTok and Pinterest connect natively. Historic customers import with their order history so email segments and lookalikes keep working from day one.
